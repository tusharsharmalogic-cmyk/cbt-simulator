📖 Guide — Yeh sab kya karta hai?
  • 📁 Question Images (Max 100): Apne questions ki images chunein. Ek image = ek question. Drop zone pe click karein ya drag & drop karein. Ctrl+A se poore folder ki saari images ek saath select kar sakte hain.
  • 🔑 Answer Key Images: Answer key ki screenshot yahan load karein (Max 3). Submit ke baad screen pe dikhegi — dekh ke manually answers fill karein.
  • 📄 Answer Key .txt file: Text file mein answers likhein (e.g. ABCDA33B). MCQ ke liye A/B/C/D, Numeric ke liye number directly likhein. Spaces automatically ignore honge. Yeh auto-fill kar deta hai submission modal mein.
  • 🔄 Auto-Advance Image: ON karne par question change hone par image bhi automatically change hoti hai. Image nav buttons disable ho jaate hain.
  • 🔑 Auto-Answer from Filename: ON karne par image ka naam (jaise 12.jpg) se txt key ka 12th answer load hota hai. Agar answer nahi hai to blank rahega.
  • 📂 Multi-Folder Mode (NEW!): Alag alag folders ki images aur unki alag ans.txt files — sab ek saath. Har folder ka apna answer key. Image number se automatic match.
  • ⏱️ Subject-wise Timer: ON karne par Physics/Maths/Chemistry ka alag timer chalta hai. Jab ek subject ka time khatam hota hai, automatically next subject pe chale jaao. Jiska time khatam, us pe wapas nahi ja sakte.
